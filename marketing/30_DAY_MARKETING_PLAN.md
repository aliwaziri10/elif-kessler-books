# Amity Falls: 30-Day Organic Marketing Plan

**Author:** Elif Kessler  
**Series:** The Amity Falls Series  
**Budget:** $0  
**Start date:** 2026-10-17

## Guardrails
- Market the actual book, not a guessed version of it.
- Book 1 is described in the project config as second-chance, slow-burn, small-town romance with memory loss, an orchard setting, and a closed-door relationship.
- Do not claim Book 1 is in Kindle Unlimited. The current project note says it is not; Books 2-4 are.
- Do not promise bestseller status, solicit positive reviews, buy reviews, or use engagement bait in groups that prohibit it.
- Use each Facebook group only if its rules permit author promotion.

## Weekly cadence
Three posts each week. Use Facebook Page first; cross-post to Instagram only after the account is set up and linked.

### Week 1
1. **Series introduction:** Introduce Elif Kessler and The Amity Falls Series. Include the series reading order.
2. **Book 1 hook:** “She comes home. He doesn't remember her.” Ask readers whether second-chance romance or memory-loss romance is the stronger hook.
3. **Reader question:** “In a second-chance romance, do you want the couple to start over, or uncover what was lost?” No purchase link needed on every post.

### Week 2
1. **Tropes:** Share the verified Book 1 tropes: second chance, slow burn, small town, return to hometown, memory loss, orchard setting, closed door.
2. **Reading order:** Post the four titles in order with a link to Book 1.
3. **Conversation:** Ask which small-town setting readers enjoy most: orchard, bookstore, diner, or old family home. Make clear this is a reader question, not a claim about the novel's setting beyond the verified orchard detail.

### Week 3
1. **Book 2 feature:** Use the existing approved project line only after checking it against the book: “A small town, an old debt, and a gift that costs a memory.”
2. **Series bridge:** Explain that the four books are part of The Amity Falls Series and share a small-town/slow-burn identity. Do not imply every book has identical tropes.
3. **Reader conversation:** “Which keeps you reading longer: an old secret, a lost memory, or a promise that was never kept?”

### Week 4
1. **Book 3 feature:** Use the current project line only after checking it against the book: “A hunger that runs in the blood and six weeks until the autumn eclipse.”
2. **Finale feature:** Introduce Book 4 as the series finale using the existing project line only after checking it against the book.
3. **Month-end post:** Re-share the series order, invite readers to follow the author page for future updates, and point new readers to Book 1.

## Weekly scorecard
Track each week:
- Posts published
- Reach / impressions
- Link clicks (if shown)
- Page follows
- Amazon orders and KENP where available
- Review count and rating shown by Amazon

Do not interpret likes as sales. Keep the content that earns meaningful link clicks and reader replies; revise content that repeatedly gets neither. With a small sample, treat results as directional, not conclusive.
