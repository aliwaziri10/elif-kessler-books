# Amity Falls: 30-Day Organic Marketing Plan

**Book:** *Where the Frost Doesn't Reach*  
**Series:** *The Amity Falls Series*  
**Budget:** $0  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-17

## Positioning

Use the verified central promise: second-chance, slow-burn small-town paranormal romance with memory loss and a supernatural mystery. Mara returns for her grandmother's funeral and finds her former fiancé Caleb alive but unable to remember the two years that included her. The orchard and the town's hidden bargain are important story elements.

Do not market this as a purely contemporary romance. Avoid spoilers about the culprit, the ending, or the final-page hook.

## Three posts per week

### Week 1: Introduce the hook
1. **Book 1 hook:** “She remembers their love. He doesn't.” Link to Book 1.
2. **Reader question:** “Would you try to rebuild a relationship if the other person had forgotten your shared past?”
3. **Series introduction:** Introduce *The Amity Falls Series* as small-town romance woven into a supernatural mystery. Add the correct reading order after verifying all four Amazon links.

### Week 2: Show the reader promise
1. **Trope post:** Second chance, slow burn, memory loss, return to hometown.
2. **Setting post:** The orchard and a valley whose protection comes at a cost. Do not explain the whole magic system.
3. **Reader question:** “Which makes a second-chance story harder: what happened before, or what one person can no longer remember?”

### Week 3: Mystery without spoilers
1. **Mystery hook:** “In Amity Falls, memory has a price. Caleb is paying for a debt that was never his.”
2. **Character focus:** Mara is a structural engineer who approaches grief and the town's secrets with a practical, investigative mind.
3. **Series bridge:** Share the series reading order and invite readers who finish Book 1 to explore the next book. Do not claim Book 1 is in Kindle Unlimited.

### Week 4: Reader conversion
1. **Book 1 reminder:** Repeat the strongest hook with a different visual from the existing assets.
2. **Series post:** Explain that Book 1 resolves its central relationship and immediate conflict while leaving a wider mystery for the series.
3. **Month-end review:** Compare clicks, page follows and sales signals. Keep posts that bring meaningful reader interaction or link clicks; revise posts that repeatedly do neither.

## Rules
- Check each group's self-promotion rules before posting.
- Do not spam groups, buy reviews, trade reviews, or request positive reviews.
- Ask readers for honest feedback without incentives.
- Do not promote a website until it is live and checked.
- Verify every book link before scheduling posts.
- Confirm the author/pen name before adding it to new graphics or bios. Project notes currently conflict on this detail.

## Weekly scorecard
Record posts published, reach, link clicks, page follows, Amazon orders, KENP for Books 2-4 where applicable, review count/rating and a same-marketplace rank snapshot. Treat small-sample results as directional, not conclusive.

## Dependencies
- Confirm author/pen name.
- Verify all four Amazon links and reading order.
- Copy current KDP description, seven keywords, categories and price before editing.
- Resolve or consciously accept the manuscript checklist's open continuity/quality items before a future manuscript revision.
