## Decision

Run a **two-day Free Book Promotion on October 24-25, 2026**, provided the KDP date picker accepts those dates and the promotion is successfully saved. Do not wait for Thanksgiving solely because it is a holiday.

Why this is the selected test:
- The book has one reported sale and one five-star review, so there is not enough data to identify a reliable sales pattern.
- Book 1 is the series entry point and Books 2-4 are in KU. A free entry book is a reasonable acquisition test, but it is not guaranteed to generate downloads, reads, reviews, or series read-through.
- A short test preserves three free days for later use in the same Select term, subject to KDP's current rules and remaining eligibility.
- There is no evidence yet that Thanksgiving would outperform October. Timing alone is not a distribution strategy.

## KDP action that still requires the account holder

I cannot operate or submit changes inside the user's KDP account from GitHub. Everything that can be prepared in the repository is prepared here; the only account action is to schedule the deal in KDP.

1. Open the KDP **Promote and advertise** page for this book.
2. Under **Run a Price Promotion**, choose **Free Book Promotion**.
3. Enter start date **October 24, 2026** and end date **October 25, 2026**.
4. Confirm the screen still shows **0/5** before saving, and check any date validation message.
5. Save/submit the promotion only after the dates are correct.
6. Return to the promotion page and verify that the promotion appears in the list with the intended dates. Do not announce the deal publicly until KDP confirms it is scheduled and the Amazon.com listing actually displays the free price.

KDP counts the start and end dates as calendar dates in Pacific Time. Use the date fields as displayed by KDP and verify the saved schedule rather than assuming the promotion is active.

## Measurement plan

Record these values before the promotion, then again on October 26 and November 2:
- Free eBook orders/downloads shown in KDP reports
- Paid eBook orders
- KENP pages read for Book 1 and Books 2-4, where available
- Review count and rating (observe only; never ask for a positive review)
- Each social post's reach, link clicks, comments, and shares if the platform provides them

Do not judge the promotion by downloads alone. The meaningful questions are whether new readers are reached, whether page reads follow, and whether Books 2-4 show any change. With a small sample, results will be directional rather than conclusive.

## Guardrails
- Do not claim the promotion is live until the Amazon listing confirms the free price.
- Do not claim downloads or revenue that are not visible in KDP reports.
- Do not buy reviews, arrange review swaps, or offer incentives for reviews.
- Do not post in reader groups unless their rules permit author promotion.
- Do not spend money on ads or paid promotion as part of this $0 test.
- Do not alter the book description, keywords, categories, or price as part of the same test. Keep the experiment interpretable.
- Do not schedule a second price promotion or Countdown Deal without rechecking the KDP page and remaining Select-term eligibility.
